About Peace O1

Peace O1 is an exploration into modern, zero-dependency digital craftsmanship. We bridge the frontier between ultra-responsive native browser APIs, refined glassmorphic aesthetics, and interactive micro-experiences.

Pure Performance

Constructed entirely without heavy frameworks, npm bloat, or complex compilation steps. Every frame renders at silky 60 FPS directly in the browser.

Glassmorphism

Subtle frosted surfaces, multi-stop radiant gradients, perspective 3D tilting, and dynamic cursor-tracking light emissions that bring pages to life.

Procedural Audio

Real-time audio synthesis powered by native Web Audio API oscillators. Gentle musical chords and chime effects without downloading audio assets.

Universal Design

Responsive geometry ready for any screen size, keyboard accessible, with persistent color auras and multilingual greetings for an inclusive web.
